An astronomical clock for the data platform. One face per environment: the sun hand is UTC, arcs on the ring are scheduled windows, spokes sit closer to the hub the fresher their data, and the figures ring the bell when something breaks. Click a face to open its system view.

    How to read the clock

    Sun hand
    Current UTC time on the 24-hour ring. Midnight at the bottom, noon at the top.
    Arcs on the ring
    Scheduled windows and incidents: blue planned, amber warning, red incident. Silver ticks are transfers to the core.
    Small suns
    Local noon for each source region (letters) and office (O1 to O3, ES for the engineering studio).
    Spokes and medallions
    Each domain spoke sits on a ray from the hub. Closer to the hub means fresher data; an amber halo means past its freshness target.
    Moon
    Ingest backlog. A full moon means the bronze yard is full.
    Star hand
    Points at the next scheduled event.
    Procession
    At the top of every hour, the apostle windows parade the runs scheduled for that hour, colored by workload.
    Niche icons
    Coins: spend rate (the stack grows with spend). Hourglass: spokes past freshness target (amber when any are late). Bell: open incidents (swings and turns red or amber). Eye: consumer activity (the pupil widens with reads).
    Calendar dial
    Releases per day this month; dots are promotions, the gold mark is month-end close.
    Rooster
    Crows just after midnight when the day passed without a red incident.
